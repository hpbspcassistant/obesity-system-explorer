Local data handling

Use fictional or de-identified personas only. Do not enter real names, contact information, or identifiable health information.

What is stored

The app stores profile names, notes, selected characteristics, variables, and connections in this browser's local storage. It does not send profile data to a server or analytics service.

How long it remains

Profiles remain on the device until they are deleted through the Profile actions menu or the browser's site data is cleared. This matters on shared, kiosk, and workshop devices.

Exports

Profile JSON exports contain the profile name, notes, characteristics, and map selections. Treat exported files according to your organisation's data-handling rules and remove them when they are no longer needed.

Deployment owner responsibility

Before production use, the deployment owner must confirm the intended audience, provide an organisational privacy contact, and decide whether additional notices or retention controls are required.
